# AI Lead Management & Qualification System

## Overview

An AI-powered lead management system built with n8n, Google Gemini, Gmail, and Google Sheets. The workflow automatically captures incoming leads, analyzes lead intent, scores lead quality, categorizes leads, detects duplicates, and sends instant notifications for high-priority opportunities.

## Features

* Automated lead capture via Webhook
* AI-powered lead qualification using Google Gemini
* Lead scoring and categorization
* Duplicate lead detection
* Hot lead email notifications
* CRM-style lead storage in Google Sheets
* End-to-end workflow automation

## Workflow

1. Lead submitted through Webhook
2. AI analyzes lead information
3. Lead score and category generated
4. Duplicate lead check performed
5. Hot leads trigger email alerts
6. All qualified leads stored in Google Sheets

## Tech Stack

* n8n
* Google Gemini AI
* Gmail API
* Google Sheets
* Webhooks

## Business Value

* Reduces manual lead qualification effort
* Improves response time for high-value leads
* Prevents duplicate lead entries
* Creates a lightweight CRM workflow
* Helps sales teams prioritize opportunities
